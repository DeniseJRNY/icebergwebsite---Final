Terms at a glance

The deal, in four lines.

Contract term

12 months, auto-renews annually. 30-day notice to exit at renewal. 12-month success fee tail on live mandates.

Fee structure

Agreed on joining. 5% of your advisory compensation per service on every Supported Transaction, from 5% to 20% depending on services used, $5K minimum, fixed for the term.

LLM and token costs

Absorbed by Iceberg. Any AI cost increases are ours to carry. Your fee percentage is locked at signing.

Your data

Always yours. Export anytime. No AI training on your contacts. Your relationships stay private, and what you contribute to the shared market is your choice.
