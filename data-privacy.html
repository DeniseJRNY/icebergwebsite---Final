Data and privacy

How your data
is handled.

Iceberg runs on two separated zones: a shared market layer everyone draws on, and a private workspace that belongs to your firm alone. Movement between them is tightly controlled. Here is exactly how, in plain terms.

Switches governing law only.
The two zones

Shared market. Private workspace.

Market · global

Who is out there

General, non-confidential facts about people and companies in the market, from trusted third-party providers and reference databases. Surfaced to your firm only at the moment you query. Never copied into your workspace.

Workspace · your firm

Who you know

Your mailboxes and LinkedIn connections, your notes and observations, and your mandates. Private to your firm, pooled across your own mandates, never shared with another firm.

What can and cannot be shared

Facts about the contact, yes. Anything about you, never.

Before any fact reaches the shared layer it is stripped of all firm, user, mandate, deal and relationship context, evaluated conservatively so that when in doubt nothing is promoted, and logged for review.

Can be shared

  • ✓"Now CFO at a company"
  • ✓"Moved from firm A to firm B"
  • ✓"Focuses on healthcare M&A"
  • ✓"Based in London"

Never shared

  • ✕Who your firm or your people are
  • ✕Any mandate, or its details
  • ✕Your intentions toward a contact, or deal terms and client identity
  • ✕How or why you know them, or how strong the relationship is
Hard limits

The lines we do not cross.

  • ✓No data is shared between workspaces.
  • ✓Mailbox and LinkedIn data never leave your workspace.
  • ✓Mandate and deal-confidential data is never promoted to the shared layer.
  • ✓The market layer is read-only to workspaces, retrieval only, never copied down.
Your rights and our commitments

Ownership, security, and continuity.

These terms apply to every engagement. Procurement-friendly by default, no enterprise tier required.

Ownership & derivative works

Your contacts, mandate materials and activity remain your property. Any Iceberg-generated enrichment tied to your contacts, including scoring and target universes, is your work product.

No AI training on your data

Your private data is used only to provide the Iceberg service to your firm. It is never used to train AI models, and never sold.

Export & deletion

Request a full export of your uploaded data and enrichments in CSV and JSON at any time. On request, Iceberg deletes within fourteen (14) days with a certified destruction attestation.

Data security & cross-border handling

Iceberg adheres to the Australian Privacy Act and Australian Privacy Principles as the primary framework, with GDPR-compliant protocols for EU-facing advisers and CCPA compliance for California-based entities.

Incident notification

In the event of a confirmed data security breach, Iceberg guarantees a 24-hour breach notification SLA from time of discovery.

Subprocessors

Iceberg uses strict-compliance subprocessors including Google Gemini, OpenAI and Exa Search, with thirty (30) days' prior written notice before adding any non-US AI provider.

Business continuity

Iceberg commits to a 4-hour Recovery Time Objective (RTO) and a 24-hour Recovery Point Objective (RPO).

Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of Queensland, Australia.
